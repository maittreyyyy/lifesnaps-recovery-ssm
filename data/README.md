# Data

`raw/` holds files from the **LifeSnaps** dataset, version 4 (Zenodo, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.7229547),
redistributed under its CC BY 4.0 license:

- `daily_fitbit_sema_df_unprocessed.csv`: daily Fitbit signals and daily mood indicators
- `hourly_fitbit_sema_df_unprocessed.csv`: hourly Fitbit signals and individual mood reports
- `personality.csv`: scored IPIP Big Five personality survey

Citation: S. Yfantidou et al., "LifeSnaps, a 4-month multi-modal dataset capturing unobtrusive
snapshots of our lives in the wild," *Scientific Data*, 9, 663, 2022.

The raw MongoDB dump (second level data) is not included. Download `rais_anonymized.zip` from
Zenodo if needed. `processed/` is generated by `src/prepare.py` and is not committed.
